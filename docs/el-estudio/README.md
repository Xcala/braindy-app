# El Estudio

Showroom of NicoMarca and his four Brains working at a round table in the Braindy studio in San Francisco.

- `studio-sf.jpg`: the photographic scene (generated with Higgsfield from `Drive/Braindy/Marca/braindy-background-SF-big.jpg`, the 3D logo mockup, the Brain 3D renders and Nicolas's photo; face and Samsung tablet fixed with masked inpainting). Current scene: Nicolas seen from behind at the front-right seat with his own white screen (his face is not shown on purpose), Builder at the head with its blue screen, a large wall clock above a smaller Braindy sign (live SF and Bogotá times are drawn over its digits in HTML) and a Café de Colombia cup at his left.
- `estudio-foto.src.html`: the live layer over the photo. Build: replace `__PHOTO__` with the photo as a data URI.
- `estudio.src.html` + `build.cjs`: the earlier illustrated version.
- `plan.html`: the plan and phases.

Rules for new images: Nicolas must look like himself (use his photos as references, crop of the face helps); no Apple products, he uses PC and Samsung.
